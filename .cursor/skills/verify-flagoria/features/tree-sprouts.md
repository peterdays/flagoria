# Tree sprouts

Where a biome holds fewer trees than its target share, a tree sprouts on a free cell of that biome (#361). `TreeSprouts` (`Scripts/mechanics/trees/tree_sprouts.gd`) runs a pass every 5 s on the host, or alone, over the 3x3 chunks around each player. Per biome in that area the target is the smaller of `BiomeCatalog.TREE_SHARE` times its land cells and the trees the untouched map grows there; a stump counts as a tree. So only trees lost for good (a structure on their stumps) are made up, and an untouched map never sprouts. Each missing tree sprouts with chance 5 s / `regrow_delay` per pass; a regrow delay of 0 turns sprouting off. A cell is skipped under a player or a mob, on the spawn, the start castle's gate or the cell outside it, on a structure, a bridge, a plant or stump, water, or beach sand with biomes on. A pass does not visit every cell: `deficits` reads the untouched map's land and tree counts from summed-area tables per 8x8 square (built once per square, dropped when the map params change) and recounts only cells a cut, sprout, structure or bridge changed; each square also lists its sprout spots per biome (cells where the untouched map could grow a tree), so when a tree is missing, `sprout_pass` decides how many sprout per biome, then looks only at those spots, squares in a random order, until they are placed. `due()` is called every 15 frames; each call counts squares not yet counted around the players for at most 2 ms (`COUNT_MS`), and a pass runs only on a call that finds every square of its areas counted, so a fresh map or a new area delays the pass by a few seconds instead of costing one frame 40 ms. The blocked cells are gathered once per pass for all players. The host sends `apply_sprout_cells(cells)` once per pass, at most 500 cells each (`Interaction.SPROUTS_PER_MESSAGE`; a bigger pass goes in several) (applied only from peer 1); sprouts are `"sprout"` entries in the cut list, so saves and joining peers get them. Shares and their derivation: [biomes.md](../../../../docs/biomes.md), Target tree share.

## Sub-features

- `sprout-target` a pass at chance 1 sprouts exactly the missing trees, more passes add none, an area still at its share after a loss sprouts none, and a chopped sprout regrows as itself.
- `sprout-blocked` no candidate cell is under a player or mob, on the spawn cell, the start castle's gate or the cell outside it, on a structure, bridge, plant, stump, water, or beach sand.
- `sprout-count` `deficits` from the per-square tables equals a count of every cell, after chops, a guard post and sprouts, on windows off the square grid, and (biomes on, in `test_sprouts_skip_blocked_cells`) next to a bridge.
- `sprout-cost` no `due()` call takes 4 ms or more headless (one may reach 6 ms) over 260 calls: on a map not counted yet until the first pass, with a second player and trees missing, and walking 40 cells a pass; about 2.2-2.5 ms at most locally.
- `sprout-sync` one `apply_sprout_cells` per pass, a client applies it only from the host and then shows the host's map; a peer joining later gets the sprouts.
- `sprout-split` a pass of more than 500 cells goes out in several `apply_sprout_cells` of at most 500 (1201 cells: 500, 500, 201).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Host, chop a few trees near the spawn, build a guard post on the stumps, and wait out about one regrow delay (300 s): new trees appear on free cells nearby, on every screen.

## Gotchas

- The tests read `BiomeCatalog.TREE_SHARE` and `TreeSprouts` through `load()`, so on a tree without them they fail with a message instead of a parse error that would hang the runner.
- The client draws all 3x3 chunks of the area before it applies the sprouts, or the cells it compares are not drawn yet.
- Counting a square costs about 0.25 ms in noise reads; a fresh 3x3 area (169 squares) takes about 20 calls (5 s), so the first pass after the map loads, a second player arriving or sprouting being turned on waits about that long. `sprout_pass` and `deficits` called directly still count missing squares at once.
- `test_sprout_deficits_match_count` passes on the old full count too: it guards the tables' equivalence, not the speed. The `PASS` line of `test_sprouts_skip_blocked_cells` is preceded by `spawn ..., gate ..., gate front ...: no candidates`.
- OCEANIC's share rounds down to 0 trees in any area, so it never sprouts.
