# Flagoria vision

> A living document. It captures where Flagoria comes from and where it is heading, and it is meant to be revised as the game evolves.

## Origins

Flagoria started about two years ago as the first iteration of a live-coded game.

## The core fascination: procedural maps

What drives Flagoria is procedural map generation: worlds that are generated, not hand-painted, so every match plays on fresh ground.

## The original concept

The first idea was capture the flag on sky islands. That turned out to be hard to build, so the game became islands with player-versus-player combat between two players.

## Multiplayer is a hard requirement

Multiplayer is essential. Every change has to keep two-player PvP working, and features that break or sideline multiplayer are out of scope.

## Current focus: a strong, cool procedural map

The priority right now is a procedural map that is genuinely strong and fun to explore, covering everything from the shapes of the islands to the elements that populate them (terrain, shorelines, vegetation, props, and how they are placed).

## Iterating on this document

This vision is a living doc. Update it when the direction changes, and keep it short.
